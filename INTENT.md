# Project Intent and Non-Authority Notice

`ams-gra-service-contract` is an independent engineering project intended to explore a machine-readable companion representation for OMS Service Contract interface information in AMS GRA-oriented toolchains.

It is **not**:

- an official AMS GRA artifact;
- an official OMS artifact;
- an official UCI artifact;
- a replacement for OMSC-TMP-003 or OMSC-INS-003;
- a certification/compliance determination;
- an assertion of endorsement by Open Arsenal, the Open Architecture Management Office, AFRL, the Department of the Air Force, the Department of Defense, or any other organization.

The project's goals are interoperability, code-generation consistency, traceability, and engineering automation.

Upstream documents remain the authoritative source for upstream requirements. If this project conflicts with an applicable official upstream artifact, users should treat that conflict as a defect or limitation in this project and follow the official source appropriate to their program/integration.

This repository should prefer linking to upstream public artifacts over copying them. Upstream content remains subject to its own notices, distribution statements, licenses, and terms.
